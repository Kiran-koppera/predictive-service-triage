Title: Sr. Incident Investigation Engineer, Automotive Log Data Analyst. Job Category: Engineering & Information Technology. Location: Fremont, California. Req. ID: 272642. Job Type: Full-time. Source: https://www.tesla.com/careers/search/job/sr-incident-investigation-engineer-automotive-log-data-analyst-272642

What to Expect

Tesla is looking for an experienced Senior Incident Investigation and Support Engineer for the Field Quality, Data Analysis and Engineering team. This role will support the Incident Investigation, Safety and Regulatory & Legal teams. The individual will be dedicated to investigations of field incidents, which includes pulling, assessing and summarizing vehicle information from CAN (Controller Area Network) logs, EDR (Event Data Recorder) data and other telemetry sources to respond to regulatory information requests. In addition, the individual will provide input for incident investigators in the field and provide feedback regarding collision performance to Engineering teams.

What You Will Do

The role assesses field incidents for compliance with regulatory reporting; compiles CAN log, EDR and other telemetry data for a given incident or incidents in order to meet information requests from regulatory authorities; provides input in discerning incident events, vehicle operation and driver inputs based on the compiled information; creates data visualizations to communicate results of data assessments; provides training and development for incident investigators in the field on vehicle logging and understanding logged values; and provides summary reports in conjunction with incident investigators for body, safety and restraints and AI team for development input and continuous vehicle improvement.

What You Will Bring

Candidates need a Bachelor's Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or proof of exceptional skills in related fields, or equivalent experience; at least 7 years of vehicle forensic analysis experience, including analysis of electronic vehicle data (CAN and EDR), incident investigation and accident reconstruction; demonstrated ability to manage databases and multiple data streams; strong analytical skills; ability to quickly learn new technologies and aptitude for understanding automotive vehicle systems; a passion for Tesla's mission and problem solving; and strong interpersonal, written, and verbal communication skills.

Compensation

91,600 to 234,000 USD annual salary plus cash and stock awards plus benefits.
